# Definition of Ready

A backlog item is ready for Sprint Planning when:

- Its type, parent Epic/Feature, user or technical value, and priority are clear.
- Scope and boundaries are understandable and small enough for a sprint.
- Acceptance criteria are observable and include relevant business-rule identifiers.
- Dependencies, risks, assumptions, and architectural questions are visible.
- Required research is completed or represented by a timeboxed Spike.
- Test and documentation expectations are understood.
- No unresolved decision prevents meaningful implementation.

Readiness supports forecasting; it is a team policy, not a mandatory Scrum artifact.
