# Estimation Guide

Story Points express relative effort, complexity, uncertainty, and integration risk; they are not hours or performance targets.

Use a Fibonacci-like scale: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, and 13. Compare work with a small reference item. Items estimated above 8 should normally be split or preceded by a Spike. Re-estimate when understanding materially changes.

Velocity may inform future forecasts only after several comparable sprints. It must not be used as an individual productivity target. Capacity, learning load, and operational work are considered during Sprint Planning.
