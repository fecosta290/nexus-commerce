# Product Vision

## Vision statement

For small and medium-sized businesses that need a coherent foundation for digital commerce, Nexus Commerce is a planned distributed platform that coordinates catalog, inventory, customer, ordering, simulated payment, promotion, notification, recommendation, file-processing, and analytics capabilities.

Unlike a production-ready commercial package, this educational project prioritizes transparent engineering trade-offs, deliberate learning, and incremental validation.

## Product goals

- Provide a consistent commerce workflow across independently bounded services.
- Preserve clear ownership of business data and service responsibilities.
- Demonstrate synchronous and asynchronous distributed communication.
- Create an observable and resilient local learning environment.
- Delay the web interface until the integrated backend is validated.

## Educational goals

The project develops architecture judgment, polyglot engineering skills, contract design, testing discipline, operational awareness, and evidence-based decision-making. The developer remains the author of all application source code.

## Success indicators

Success is demonstrated by reviewed requirements, traceable decisions, reliable integration behavior, developer-authored implementations, and reproducible local validation. No implementation or operational success is claimed at the current Planning / Sprint 0 stage.
